## 🏛️ About

**Heritage by Design** helps cultural institutions move from passive exhibition models to community-centred innovation programmes.

Using Living Lab methods and a structured co-design toolkit, we support organisations in connecting collections, places and people with today's social challenges. Participation stops being an isolated event and becomes a repeatable way of working.

This repository hosts the **website** of the project. It is currently in the prototype stage.

## 🧭 What we do

| | Phase | In short |
|:-:|---|---|
| **01** | **Understand** | Map the institution, its audiences and local stakeholders through a readiness audit |
| **02** | **Co-Design** | Facilitate workshops with boards, canvases and cards that bring experts and citizens to the same table |
| **03** | **Experiment** | Test ideas through small, low-tech pilots in real-life settings |
| **04** | **Evaluate** | Assess every idea for feasibility, sustainability, scalability and replicability |

## ✨ Services

- 🔍 **Readiness audit**: where participation can start and what it needs to grow
- 🧩 **Strategic co-design workshops**: shared institutional and audience goals
- 🏆 **Community engagement challenges**: school challenges, photography dialogues and more
- 🚶 **Participatory experience design**: accessible journeys and low-tech engagement formats
- 📊 **Social impact & RRI reporting**: transparent, ethical and responsible measurement
